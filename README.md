## About Me

I'm a **Ph.D. Chemical Engineer** and **digital leader** with 10+ years of experience at the intersection of **pharmaceutical R&D**, **data science**, and **Agentic AI**. Currently, I lead Takeda's R&D FAIR Data Strategy and Digital Connectivity — building enterprise-scale platforms that make research data trustworthy, automated, and AI-ready.

My work spans from deploying production Agentic AI systems for ontology curation to developing Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) and digital twins for continuous manufacturing — always bridging cutting-edge data science with real-world pharmaceutical applications.

---

## What I'm Working On

- **FAIR Data Governance at Scale** — Building automated, policy-driven data governance across Takeda's R&D ecosystem
- **Agentic AI for Science** — Designing multi-agent systems for ontology curation, entity registration, and stewardship automation
- **Scientific Machine Learning** — Publishing notebooks and tools for PINNs, mechanistic modeling, and hybrid ML/physics systems in biopharma
- **AI for Drug Discovery** — Cheminformatics, NLP on scientific literature, and AI-guided process development
